Build my voice

Want future edits to feel more like your writing? Share 3–5 favourite pieces you've written, or relevant copy for the project. One piece is enough to start. Tell me which are yours, which are writing you admire, and where you'll use the voice: email, essays, LinkedIn, or a named project.

I'll build a small style guide from how you open, develop and finish a piece, its rhythm, diction, humour and emotional tone. Admired writing stays labelled as inspiration. Each writer keeps a separate profile, and sample facts stay out of other drafts.

To let me remember your later corrections and stated preferences, say “Keep learning my voice.” You only need to opt in once. You can pause learning, inspect what's saved, forget a sample or preference, restore an earlier version, or export the guide whenever you like.
