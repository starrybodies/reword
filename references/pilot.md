# Try Reword in your own agent

Use the installed skill in your own harness and with your own model. This pilot
collects your experience of using Reword, not a blind comparison or evidence of
superiority over another tool. A formal comparison can follow separately.

1. Follow [INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md). Confirm version `0.5.2`, then invoke
   `/reword` in Claude Code, `$reword` in Codex, or select Reword with `@` in a
   supported ChatGPT skill interface.
2. Supply three deliberately chosen pieces that you wrote. Pick pieces that
   sound like you and are relevant to the writing you want help with. Label the
   destination of each. Use other people's writing only as clearly marked
   reference inspiration; it cannot establish your authored voice.
3. Ask Reword to build a private voice collection using those three samples.
   Keep the samples, drafts and voice store in your own harness and chosen
   private storage. Do not send them to the marketing site or include them in
   feedback. Your harness/provider's normal processing and privacy terms apply.
4. Try three real tasks: a short text message, a professional email or cover
   letter, and one other piece you actually need to write. Supply the facts and
   intended reader for each. Ask Reword to use the same voice with the
   appropriate destination conventions. Preserve originals and review the
   proposed edits and source comparison.
5. Make one deliberate revision yourself. Tell the agent what you changed and
   why; ask it to save that revision once and refresh the relevant voice guide.
   Use the refreshed guide on a short follow-up. Saving this one correction
   does not require enabling ongoing learning. Enable that separately only if
   you want it.
6. Return the concise feedback below through the feedback route offered by the
   site. Keep the actual writing and voice collection private.

Suggested starting request:

> Use Reword to build my voice from these three chosen authored samples. Keep
> the collection private in this harness. Then help with three real tasks: a text
> message, a professional email, and one other piece I need to write, using only
> the facts I supply. I will make one deliberate revision for you to learn from;
> do not enable ongoing learning unless I ask.

## Feedback

```text
Reword version: 0.5.2
Harness and version:
Model (as shown in the harness):
Installation issues, or "none":
Did the writing sound like me? 1–5 (1 = not at all; 5 = strongly):
What changed after my deliberate revision:
Would I keep using Reword? yes / maybe / no — why:
```

Report what you observed, including uncertainty. Do not invent ratings,
comparisons or test results. Share no samples, drafts, names or confidential
details merely to explain a rating. For voice controls and consent, see
[voice collections](voice-collection.md).
